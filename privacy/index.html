Privacy policy

Itachi Tech · Last updated 12 November 2026

This page covers Itachi Tech itself: this website, our feedback form, emails you send us, and the small internal tools we use to run the studio. Each app has its own privacy page with the details for that app:

  • Music Player

We never sell your information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not use it for anything other than what is written here.

This website

This website is a set of plain pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no sign-in. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address to keep the service secure. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Feedback form

Our feedback form is a Google Form. You choose what to send: the app, a star rating, a topic, details, your phone model and, only if you want a reply, your email. We use it only to fix and improve our apps. Answers are kept in our Google account and never shared with anyone else. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Forms.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you. We keep the conversation in our mailbox and delete it if you ask.

Our internal tools

We use a private tool, "Odyssey", that signs in with our own Google account to read our own reports from Google Play and AdMob (for example installs, ratings and ad earnings, shown as totals). These reports do not identify app users. The tool is only used by Itachi Tech; it never asks you to sign in and never accesses your Google account.

Odyssey's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website, the feedback form or email. If you think a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. Email itachi.tech16@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this page changes, we update the date at the top.
