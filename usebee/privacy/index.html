01What stays on your phone

  • The items you add (name, date, place, date type, reminder, quantity), your settings and your reminders are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Camera pictures are processed on your phone (Google ML Kit) to read the barcode and the printed date. They are not stored and not sent anywhere.
  • If you use "Back up to a file", the file is saved where you choose. We never receive it.
  • Expiry reminders are created on your phone.
  • If Android backup is on, Android may copy the app's data to your own Google account. That backup is yours, handled by Google. We can't see it, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Why
